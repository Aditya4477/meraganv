About Theog

Play Archive Film

Theog is a historic town and tehsil perched high in the Shimla hills, sitting at 2,310 meters above sea level and 28 kilometers eastward from the state capital.

With first recorded settlements dating back to 1902, Theog emerged as an indispensable trading hub and gateway along the historic trade tracks to Tibet. Defined by its unique amphitheatre of five distinct ghats (ridges)—Premghat, Rahighat, Deorighat, Janoghat, and Bagaghat—it has preserved centuries of indigenous Kath-Kuni architectural wisdom, apple cultivation, and oral histories.

The Character of Theog

01 / Craftsmanship

Kath-Kuni Architecture

Alternating bands of cedar deodar wood and dry stones laid without mortar. This earthquake-resistant engineering allows temples and homes to flex safely across centuries.

02 / Geography & Sacred Ridges

Mountain Contours

High crests and deep pine ravines forming micro-climates where mountain shrines, terraced orchards, and village clans flourish together.

03 / Agrarian Cycles

High-Altitude Orchards

Snow-fed streams nourish terraced apple and potato cultivation. April turns the hills white with delicate blossoms, followed by deep amber harvests.

04 / Oral Memory

Living Memory & Devtas

Village elders safeguard oral ballads, trade route memories, and the sacred palanquin rituals of local mountain deities.
